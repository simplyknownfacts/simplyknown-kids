🏆 Trophy visual directions — pick one before coding

Same 5-tier ladder in all three rows (Star chip → Medal → Cup → Crowned cup → Gem trophy) — only the STYLE changes. Rarity is meant to be obvious from across the room: each tier bigger, shinier, and busier than the last.

⚠️ Shown small and side-by-side on purpose, so all three directions can be compared at once. The real in-app celebration fills much more of the screen — the "Gem trophy" cell here at 132px becomes roughly 40-50% of a phone's width when it's the actual centerpiece, not a comparison thumbnail. These are vector shapes drawn in code (free, instant to iterate) — not painted or rendered art. If the pick needs a more dimensional/painted look than code can give, that's a separate art-generation decision (Comfy/Nano Banana), parked pending Scott's route call.

A — Chunky Toy

Thick black outline, flat bright colour, rounded and bouncy. Matches the app's playful chunky-UI direction (Fredoka-style buttons already used elsewhere). Reads instantly even tiny; least "shiny," most toy-shelf.

B — Storybook Shine

Soft painterly gradients, warm glow, matches the app's default Storybook night theme. Feels earned and precious rather than loud — the risk is it may read as too gentle for "spectacular."

C — Arcade Blast

High-contrast metallic gradient, hard rim-light, radiating burst behind the top two tiers. Loudest and most "I won something huge" — closest to Scott's "spectacular," closest visual cousin to the app's existing Neon Arcade theme.
