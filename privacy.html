SIMPLYKNOWN KIDS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

The short version

  • SimplyKnown Kids is a learning-and-play web app for young children, set up and managed by a parent.
  • By default, everything (your child profiles, settings, and progress) is stored only on your device.
  • Optional cloud sync lets you use the app on more than one device. If you turn it on, your child profiles and settings are stored on our sync service so they can travel between your devices.
  • No ads. No third-party ad tracking. We never sell or rent your information.
  • You can delete your data at any time by removing profiles, clearing the site data, or contacting us.

Who we are

This policy covers the SimplyKnown Kids web app at kids.simplyknown.co (registered with Yoto as "Valiant Breeze Kids App"). Questions: simplyknownfacts@gmail.com.

Information the app handles

Stored on your device default

When a parent sets up the app, the following is saved in your browser's local storage and stays on that device unless you enable cloud sync:

  • Child profiles: first name (or nickname), birthday/age, and a chosen avatar and color.
  • Preferences you set: voice choice, mascot, which activities and features are turned on, and any YouTube channels you add to the Watch section.
  • In-app progress: activity counts, achievements/ribbons, and streaks.

Optional cloud sync opt-in

If you choose to create a sync account (with an email address and password), we store a copy of your child profiles and the settings listed above on our sync service (run on Cloudflare Workers) so you can use the same setup across devices. This includes the child's name, birthday/age, avatar, preferences, achievements, and the YouTube channels you added. You can sign out or stop syncing at any time in Parent Settings, and you can remove synced data by deleting profiles (the change is saved to the cloud copy too).

Yoto connection opt-in

If you connect a Yoto account, you sign in on Yoto's own page and grant the app read-only access to your family's Yoto card library so the app can show your cards and play them. The Yoto access token is stored only on your device; we never see or store your Yoto password, and the app does not send your Yoto data to anyone else. You can disconnect Yoto at any time in Parent Settings.

Audio and voices

Spoken prompts, encouragement, and character voices are pre-recorded audio files that ship with the app. The app does not record, store, or transmit your child's voice or use the device microphone.

Embedded YouTube

The optional Watch section embeds videos from YouTube channels a parent chooses. When a video plays, it is delivered by YouTube, and YouTube's own privacy practices apply to that embedded content. We do not receive viewing data from YouTube.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no third-party advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not sell, rent, or trade your information.
  • We do not build advertising profiles of you or your child.

Children's privacy

SimplyKnown Kids is intended for use by young children under the direction of a parent or guardian. A parent creates and manages each child profile. The limited information collected (a name or nickname, age, avatar, and preferences) is used only to run the app for that child, never for advertising. A parent can review, change, or delete a child's profile and data at any time, or contact us to request deletion.

Data retention and deletion

On-device data remains until you delete profiles or clear the site/app data in your browser. If you use cloud sync, your synced copy is updated when you change profiles; to remove it entirely, delete your profiles while signed in, or email us to request deletion of your synced account data.

Security

Cloud sync uses an authenticated key tied to your sign-in. Yoto and sync tokens are kept in your device's local storage. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what is collected and avoid sharing it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
