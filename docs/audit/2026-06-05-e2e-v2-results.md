Two independent methods. **Method A** = scripted assertions + screenshots (node Playwright, all 19 activities × tiers × phone+tablet, live). **Method B** = per-tier-band agents clicking the live site and judging by eye. **Headline:** the app's core is independently confirmed solid; **2 real product bugs found + fixed** (ship in v98); a handful of content/calibration items logged for your review.

## Method A — scripted (asserts the real outcome, not just "no crash")
- All 19 activities + shell (picker/home/3 hubs/achievements) + settings gate, across tiers (body-parts all 8) × phone(390×844)+tablet(820×1180), live.
- **Body Parts (v97 fix) — 32/32** all 8 tiers × phone+tablet: correct part → flash + "Yes! That's the {part}"; wrong → shake + names the part tapped + **never a false "Yes"** + no advance.
- **Tier-1 auto-play** (hello-colors cycle, animal garden, peek-a-boo, count-along) — **12/12**: each self-progresses (text changes / audio fires), not a dead screen.
- **Rotation bug** caught (8 failures pre-fix). _Honest note:_ the first detector passed trivially (hollow green — the v1 failure mode); rewrote it to assert the canvas **fills the window after rotating**, then it correctly failed the 4 buggy activities and passed magic-touch. `grep` for resize handlers confirmed the root cause independently.
- Harness: `tests/e2e/v2/` (re-runnable; `npm i playwright`, browsers cached; `BASE_URL` overridable).

## Method B — agent-driven (4 tier-band agents)
- **2-4y band (live clicks): all PASS** — shape-match drag (correct→matched, wrong→snap-back+boop), abcs spell (wrong letter rejected, C-A-T advances), days quiz, math add/subtract (correct arithmetic, wrong rejected), money count, animal-sounds quiz (green/red + advance), hello-colors strict quiz, body-parts standard + **wheelchair (nose≠ear confirmed)**, no-profile→picker redirect, PIN gate. Found body-07 misalignment (#2).
- **6-8y band (source-verified — browser had died):** tier gating correct; math ×/+ , count-along skip-count, days ordinal/relative, money count logic all sound; all shared audio fns resolve (no missing-function crashes). Surfaced content items #3–9.
- **0-2y & 4-6y bands: blocked** — the shared Playwright **browser backend timed out** (`browserBackend.callTool: Timeout`) under the long multi-agent run; the agents correctly refused to fabricate results. **Not a product bug** (site returned HTTP 200 throughout). Coverage backfilled by Method A (load + 0 errors at those tiers) + the tier-1 auto-play checks above + the quiz/match/spell engines being live-confirmed at 2-4y and source-verified at 6-8y (tiers 5-6 reuse the same engines).
