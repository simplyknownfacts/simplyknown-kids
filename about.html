SIMPLYKNOWN KIDS

A calm, ad-free play & learning app for little kids

Games, early learning, art, and the family's own Yoto cards — in one parent-controlled web app.

SimplyKnown Kids (registered with Yoto as "Valiant Breeze Kids App") is a free web app for toddlers and young children, roughly ages 1 to 8. A parent sets up a profile for each child, and the app adjusts the activities to that child's age. It installs like an app, works offline, and has no advertising.

What's inside

🎮 Games
Simple, tactile games — tap, pop, match, and a piano you can slide your finger across.
📚 Learn
ABCs, counting, colors, shapes, animals, days, money, and more.
🎨 Art
Finger paint, stamps, color-splash, and color-in scenes.
🎧 Listen (Yoto)
Connect your Yoto account to browse and play your own Yoto cards in the app.
🏅 Ribbons
Gentle rewards that celebrate effort and keep kids encouraged.
👨‍👩‍👧 Parent controls
PIN-protected settings: pick activities, voices, and limits per child.

How it uses Yoto

Families who own Yoto can connect their Yoto account in Parent Settings. The app requests read-only access to your family's card library so it can show your cards as a friendly grid and play them, with simple now-playing controls (play, pause, previous and next chapter). The app never changes your library and never sees your Yoto password. You can disconnect at any time.

Built for families

  • No ads and no third-party ad tracking.
  • Child profiles and settings stay on your device by default, with optional cloud sync across your devices.
  • A parent is always in control of what each child can see and do.
